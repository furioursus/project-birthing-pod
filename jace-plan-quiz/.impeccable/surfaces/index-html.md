# Surface brief: the quiz (landing, questions, result, sheet index)

Mode: Experience. The work leads from the first viewport. The question flow runs under Operate rules: one decision per screen, fast and clear.

Audience and job: lore fans take the quiz and post the result. Non-players arrive from a shared link, often straight to one result. Success means equal parts posting and reading.

Constraints:
- PRODUCT.md applies.
- The blue-mana palette is pinned.
- Mind-reading with an illusion fake-out is pinned.
- Canon facts are untouched. Jokes live only in the framing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Jace's plans are literal plans. The quiz is a cyanotype blueprint of a mind palace. Jace surveys it while you answer, confidently misreads you, redlines his mistake and corrects it. It refuses the category default: a stack of answer cards on a gradient in a centred column.

OWN-WORLD:
- The ground is a drenched cyanotype blue sheet with a faint white grid.
- White single-weight linework and a drawing frame with zone markers.
- A title block, grid bubbles for answer keys, and diagonal hatching for surveyed rooms.
- Redline-red pencil corrections and revision clouds.
- Pale-cyan pencil notes in architect's hand lettering.
- Big Shoulders for condensed drafting display. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading text.

STORY: A visitor learns that Jace is a genius who keeps out-thinking himself. They get surveyed, watch Jace's flattering illusion get redlined, laugh at their real plan, share the sheet, and read the field report.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Landing: two columns. The entry holds the redlined headline ("BRILLIANT" struck through, "poorly thought-out" pencilled in), a one-line lede and the front door, the "Formulate a plan" button with a door-swing arc. Jace's pencil underdrawing sits behind it.
- Beside the entry is the palace plan: a radial map of Jace's mind in the manner of a Krakoan data page. A central chamber sits inside a ring corridor, ringed by fourteen numbered chambers sized by plan rating.
- Below both is a dot-leadered legend that redacts every plan the visitor hasn't opened, so the map teases without spoiling.
- On mobile everything stacks, and the door button stays in the first viewport.
- Result: a single sheet with the title, the stamp, a scale-bar rating, the plan in Jace's pencil, and Share, all above the fold.

Signature interaction:
- After the last answer, the floor strip finishes and the pencil notes "Reading you…".
- The sheet stamps the opposite outcome APPROVED.
- A red revision cloud draws around it with the note "REV 1: illusion", and the real plan redraws.
- It can be skipped. Reduced motion goes straight to the real result.
- Motion grammar: linework draws on with exponential ease-out. Nothing else performs.

FORM: Blueprint of a mind palace. Candidate 5 of 7, seed key 9df6c40d, degraded roll with no challengers.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
