## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** the skin is specific, but the skeleton is generic. The authored parts:
- The hedron diamonds work as both the progress bar and the "Plan rating" meter. That's lore-literate without using card art.
- The case-file framing and the rubber stamp ("BEEFED IT" / "IT ACTUALLY WORKED").
- The mono-blue palette suits a blue-mana telepath.
- Fraunces at display size looks good.

Underneath, it's the standard quiz template: a centred 44rem column, an uppercase mono eyebrow on every screen, a serif H1, a muted lede, a pill CTA, a stack of numbered answer cards, pill chips and a radial glow on navy. Change the copy and a Hogwarts-house quiz could ship it unchanged, apart from the diamonds. Nothing in the interaction plays on telepathy, illusion or mind-sculpting, and that's the joke the whole product is built on.

Neobrutalism: not present. There's one tilted bordered stamp, and the case-file metaphor justifies it.

## Overall Impression

The writing is the best thing here: tight voice, jokes in the framing, sourced facts. The structure is solid too. The design doesn't match that confidence yet. Two things let it down:
- **The quiz forgets you.** Refresh resets it, and Back wipes it.
- **The ending isn't a moment.** The result appears instantly as a wiki entry, Share is below the fold, and every shared link unfurls with the same card.

Biggest opportunity: make the result page into a verdict that fits in one phone screenshot.

## What's Working

1. **The hedron motif carries meaning.** Progress and rating share one lore-native glyph. The meter has `role="img"` and an "N out of 10" label.
2. **The copy follows the brief.** "Formulate a plan", "Rethink the last one", "Share your shame" and "Lavinia deserves a raise" are all framing. "What happened" stays factual, and each result links its source and Scryfall evidence. The "Your plan is…" vs "Case file" split for people arriving from a shared link is a smart touch.
3. **The accessibility basics are solid:**
   - Focus moves to the heading on each screen, and the focus ring is a visible 3px.
   - `prefers-reduced-motion` kills the stamp animation.
   - Answer order is shuffled.
   - Text contrast passes everywhere (muted text 8.3:1, beef 6.5:1, triumph 10.8:1).
   - No horizontal overflow at 390px or at 200% zoom.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Progress dies on refresh or Back**
- **Why it matters:**
  - Picks live only in memory (`main.ts:46-60`), and `#/quiz` is a single history entry that `beginQuiz()` resets.
  - Refresh sends you back to Q1, and the Back gesture drops you on the intro with everything gone.
  - For a phone user who gets interrupted mid-quiz, that's the main way to lose them.
- **Fix:**
  - Encode picks in the route (`#/quiz/2-0-3`) or sessionStorage, and restore them in `beginQuiz`.
  - Push one history entry per question so Back means "previous question".
  - Guard the hotkeys with `if (event.repeat) return`.
- **Command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The result isn't a peak, and sharing is structurally weak**
- **Why it matters:**
  - For a joke quiz, the result screenshot or link is the product.
  - The reveal is instant. The stamp, rating, plan and Share sit in one long column, with Share about 400px below the fold on mobile.
  - `index.html` has `og:title` and `og:description` but no `og:image`. Results are hash routes, so every share unfurls the same generic card.
  - A 0/10 rating renders as ten near-invisible outlines, so that built-in punchline disappears.
- **Fix:**
  - Put a verdict block above the fold: title, stamp, rating, the plan in one line, and Share.
  - Add a short "reading your mind…" beat before the reveal, gated on reduced motion.
  - Move to path routes (`/plan/<id>`) with prerendered per-result OG tags and images. Netlify can serve those.
- **Commands:** `/impeccable layout`, `/impeccable delight`, `/impeccable animate`

**[P2] The visual language is category-interchangeable**
- **Why it matters:**
  - The content is highly specific and the frame is a template.
  - The eyebrow appears on every screen (the detector confirms it twice), alongside the radial glow and pills everywhere.
  - The plan quote uses a side-tab stripe on a rounded card.
  - The desktop landing is a paragraph above an empty half-screen.
- **Fix:**
  - Commit to one metaphor and carry it through. Options: a real dossier (file tabs, redaction bars, typed labels), or a hedron constellation that assembles as you answer.
  - Drop the eyebrow from the question screens; "Question 5 of 10" already duplicates the diamonds.
  - Replace the side-tab on `.plan` and `.plan-card` with something native to the metaphor.
- **Commands:** `/impeccable bolder`, `/impeccable shape`

**[P2] Screen-reader and input hardening**
- **Why it matters:** these all affect Sam directly.
  - `<main aria-live="polite">` combined with heading focus re-announces the whole screen on every answer, and the whole article on the result.
  - "Question n of 10" is read twice.
  - The stamp's `aria-label` sits on a plain div, so it's ignored.
  - Empty diamonds (1.73:1) and card borders (1.51:1) fail the 3:1 minimum for non-text UI.
  - `.answer:hover` isn't wrapped in `@media (hover:hover)`, so on touch the next question's answer can look pre-selected.
- **Fix:**
  - Remove `aria-live` from `main`.
  - Drop the duplicate label.
  - Give the stamp `role="img"` or make it plain text.
  - Raise `--line` to about 3:1.
  - Gate hover styles on `@media (hover:hover)`.
- **Commands:** `/impeccable harden`, `/impeccable audit`

**[P2] Lore jargon isn't glossed for non-players**
- **Why it matters:** "What happened" names things like the Infinite Consortium, the Sylex, compleated, Realmbreaker and the Echoverse with no explanation. The title "I Expected as Much" is never attributed, and the Evidence chips aren't explained. A non-player reaches the punchline and doesn't get it.
- **Fix:**
  - Add short inline glosses.
  - Attribute the flavor-text quote.
  - Add a one-line caption for the Evidence chips.
  - Optionally open with a two-line summary before the full canon.
- **Command:** `/impeccable clarify`

## Minor Observations

- On first load the CTA is focused programmatically, so mouse users see a double ring and it looks pressed.
- "Link copied" never resets. Desktop Chromium has no `navigator.share`, so desktop always falls back to the clipboard.
- The plans index doesn't highlight your own result.
- "Every Jace plan, in order" is in publication order, not story order. Say which.
- An unknown plan id silently shows the intro instead of a "no such case file" message.
- The footer runs to about 118 characters per line, and result paragraphs to about 88 on desktop. Cap them at about 70ch.
- Dark-only is a fine choice for this mood, and `theme-color` matches it.
- The project rules are met: Fan Content Policy footer, no bundled card art, a source link per result.

## Questions to Consider

- Jace is a telepath. Why does the quiz *ask*? What if it visibly reads you: hedrons assemble as you answer, it shows a confident wrong result as an illusion, then reveals the real one?
- Is the result a case file or a verdict? What does the screenshot people would actually post look like, and does it fit on one phone screen?
- Would a non-player read all of "What happened"? Should the facts be a two-line setup for the punchline, with the full canon one tap deeper?
- Ten questions with no reaction in between: what would one in-voice aside around Q5 do for that dip, while keeping the jokes in the framing and out of the facts?
